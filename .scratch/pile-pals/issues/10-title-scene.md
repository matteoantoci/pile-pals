# 10: Title scene

Spec: [spec.md](../spec.md)

**What to build:** The app opens on a live title scene. Pals hop and fall into small piles. The Child taps a pal to make it jump, and taps the green arrow button to play.

**Blocked by:** 05 (Card sound effects), 08 (Group and level celebrations)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Animated pals hop and fall into 3 small piles in a loop.
- [ ] The logo is a card pile with eyes, with no text.
- [ ] One large green round button with a white arrow starts the level.
- [ ] A tap on a pal makes it jump and play a sound. Pals with no own sound play a pop.
- [ ] The first touch on the title scene unlocks audio.
- [ ] A restored level skips the title scene.
- [ ] An app update applies on the title scene before the first touch.
